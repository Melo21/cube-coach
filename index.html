Notation guide
RRight column up (right face clockwise, as if you faced it). R'Same face, counterclockwise: right column down. R2Half turn, either way. LLeft column down. L' is up. UTop row to the left. U' is to the right. DBottom row to the right. D' is to the left. FFront face clockwise. BBack face: its top row moves left, seen from the front.
